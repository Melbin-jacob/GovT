# SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — Directive on Example Widgets (not a real policy)
# 6. Requirements
# 6.1 Registration

6.1.1 Each department must register every widget before it is put into service.

6.1.2 The register must record the owner, the location and the model of each widget.

# 6.2 Calibration

6.2.1 Widgets should be calibrated at least once a year.

6.2.2 Calibration results must be reviewed by a qualified technician.

6.2.3 Faulty widgets must be removed from service until they are repaired.

# 6.3 Records

6.3.1 Operators must log every calibration and must retain the log for two years.

6.3.2 The widget register may be published on the department's website.

# 6.4 Transparency and reporting

6.4.1 Clients are informed when a widget is used to process their request.

6.4.2 Departments report annually on the number of widgets in service.

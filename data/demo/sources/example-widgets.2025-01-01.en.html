Directive on Example Widgets

6. Requirements

6.1 Registration

  • 6.1.1 Each department must register every widget before it is put into service.
  • 6.1.2 The register must record the owner, the location, the model and the firmware version of each widget.

6.2 Risk assessment

  • 6.2.1 Departments must complete a widget risk assessment before a widget is put into service.
  • 6.2.2 The widget risk assessment must be updated when the purpose of the widget changes.

6.3 Calibration

  • 6.3.1 Widgets must be calibrated at least once a year.
  • 6.3.2 Calibration results must be reviewed by a qualified technician.
  • 6.3.3 Faulty widgets must be removed from service until they are repaired.

6.4 Records

  • 6.4.1 Operators must log every calibration.
  • 6.4.2 Operators must retain the calibration log for two years.

6.5 Transparency and reporting

  • 6.5.1 Clients are informed when a widget is used to process their request.
  • 6.5.2 Departments report annually on the number of widgets in service.
    • The report covers the fiscal year.

Appendix A: Definitions

widget: an example device used only in test data.
